OpenAQ envisions a world where everyone breathes healthy air, enabled by universal access to data. To date, we have operated our API with a high degree of flexibility, prioritizing ease of entry while gently enforcing our rate limit policy. However, as our community of users has grown, so has the demand on our infrastructure.
