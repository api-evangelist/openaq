An OpenAQ Community Impact Story by Matt Lane Mozambique in southwest Africa is known for its pristine 1,500 mile coastline with its more than 2,000 species of fish. Many of its 30+ million residents are multi-lingual, and there are 40+ indigenous languages and dialects (Portuguese is the official language). Agriculture is at the heart of Mozambique’s economy and employs more than 80% of the labor force.
