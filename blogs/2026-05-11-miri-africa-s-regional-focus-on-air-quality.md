An OpenAQ Partner Profile by Matt Lane OpenAQ , the world’s largest open-source, open-access air quality data platform, is pleased to announce that Miri Africa is now an OpenAQ air sensor partner . This signifies that Miri Africa supports customer data sharing via OpenAQ and we have a joint technical integration implemented. I recently sat down with Miri Africa’s founder and CEO, Temidayo Oniosun, to discuss the company’s goals, products, and customers.
