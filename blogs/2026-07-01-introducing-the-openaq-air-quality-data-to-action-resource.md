OpenAQ is thrilled to share a new directory of resources geared toward, but not limited to, folks monitoring air quality. The Air Quality “Data-to-Action” Resource Directory includes: Web-based resources related to installing and managing air quality monitoring networks and turning data into action A roster of organizations that provide technical support along the “Data-to-Action” spectrum A roster of equipment manufacturers that provide technical assistance Instructions for suggesting new content are found on the About tab of this evolving directory.
